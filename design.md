# SkinSolve — Design Tokens & UI/UX System

## 1. Brand Palette
- **Background**: Warm Off-White (`#FAF8F5`, `bg-[#FAF8F5]`)
- **Primary Accent**: Botanical Deep Sage (`#2D5A43`, `text-[#2D5A43]`, `bg-[#2D5A43]`)
- **Primary Hover**: Rich Pine (`#224433`)
- **Soft Accent / Badges**: Sage Mint Light (`#E8EFE9`, `#D1E0D4`)
- **Secondary Accent**: Soft Warm Terracotta / Sand (`#E5A985`, `#F3EDE2`)
- **Typography Primary**: Rich Charcoal (`#1A201C`)
- **Typography Muted**: Muted Slate/Olive (`#5A6860`)
- **Borders & Dividers**: Subtle Sage Gray (`#E4E8E4`)
- **Cards**: Clean Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle shadow `shadow-[0_4px_24px_-4px_rgba(45,90,67,0.06)]`

## 2. Layouts
- **Hero & Landing**: High impact editorial aesthetic with typography, interactive routine preview, problem statement, key benefits.
- **Onboarding Flow**: 5-step intuitive wizard with step pills, smooth card transitions, micro-badges, and live cost calculation.
- **AM / PM Routine View**: Clear category progression (01 Cleanser -> 02 Treatment -> 03 Moisturizer -> 04 Sunscreen).
- **Match Score Card**: Circular / bar visualization of the 6-factor scoring breakdown.
